\section{Remaining work}
\label{sec:remaining}

In priority order, with the decision each one informs.

\paragraph{1. Close the composition question.} Evaluate \texttt{AUGNEG} and
\texttt{AUGNEGS1}. If the composed arm lands near $\AugNegPrediction$ the
levers are independent and the recipe is additive; if it lands near the better
single lever they share a mechanism, and the report's conclusion becomes
sharper rather than weaker. \emph{Blocked only on GPU availability.}

\paragraph{2. Signed channel-wise decay.} Implement the complex-eigenvalue
variant of \citet{ckda2026} on top of \texttt{allow\_neg\_eigval} and train one
arm against \texttt{D1}. This is the direction the per-horizon evidence points
at, the implementation is a modification of kernels already in use, and the
pre-registered prediction is specific: the gain should concentrate on seasonal
datasets and grow with horizon. \emph{The most promising remaining experiment.}

\paragraph{3. Measure \texttt{V2L}'s own annealing point.} The fifteen
snapshots exist; scoring them on the validation split costs one GPU-hour and
removes the one confound in the \texttt{V2C}/\texttt{V2M} pair that carries the
``looping does not survive v2'' claim (\S\ref{sec:protocol}).

\paragraph{4. Probabilistic evaluation.} Read out the full $97$-row CRPS of the
quantile head. Note that MSIS is not comparable: it needs the $0.025$ and
$0.975$ quantiles, which the nine-level head does not produce.

\paragraph{5. Enumerated sampler with augmentation.} The v2 sampler and
\texttt{--augment} are currently mutually exclusive by an explicit guard, which
is conservative rather than fundamental --- the enumerated path does build a
per-sample Philox generator. Lifting it (roughly twenty lines) is what makes a
genuine three-lever arm possible.

\paragraph{6. Adaptive $k$ at inference.} Untested, and the only remaining
version of the looping hypothesis that the current results do not speak to: if
the value of looping is per-input adaptivity rather than depth, a fixed-$k$
sweep could not have found it.
